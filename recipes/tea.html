TEA RECIPES

Description

Indian Tea, commonly known as Chai, is a beloved hot beverage enjoyed across the country and beyond. It’s a comforting blend of black tea, milk, sugar, and aromatic spices like cardamom, ginger, and cloves. Each region — and even each household — has its own unique twist on how it’s made. Chai is more than just tea — it's a daily ritual, a symbol of hospitality, and a perfect companion for conversations, monsoons, and peaceful mornings. Rich, flavorful, and spiced just right, Indian Chai is a true hug in a cup..

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 cup water
  • 1–2 tsp black tea leaves (or 1 tea bag)
  • 1 cup milk (whole or toned, as preferred)
  • 1–2 tsp sugar (adjust to taste)
  • ½ inch piece of fresh ginger (crushed)
  • 2–3 green cardamom pods (crushed)

STEPS

  1. Boil 1 cup of water in a saucepan..
  2. AAdd crushed ginger and cardamom pods. Let it simmer for 1–2 minutes to release flavor.
  3. Add 1–2 tsp black tea leaves (or a tea bag) and simmer for 2 minute .
  4. Pour in 1 cup of milk and add sugar to taste. Stir well..
  5. Let it boil and rise once, then turn off the heat.
  6. Strain into a cup and serve hot.
