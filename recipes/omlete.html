OMLETE RECIPES

Description

An omelette is a classic and versatile egg dish that's quick and easy to make. It's made by beating eggs and cooking them in a frying pan until they’re set, then optionally folding in fillings like cheese, vegetables, or meats. Perfect for breakfast, brunch, or even a light dinner, the omelette can be customized to suit any taste. Whether you prefer it plain, cheesy, or loaded with your favorite ingredients, it’s a great go-to recipe.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 or 3 large eggs
  • Salt (to taste)
  • Black pepper (to taste)
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil

STEPS

  1. Beat 2–3 eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper in a bowl.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp of butter or oil in a pan over medium heat, then pour in the eggs.
  3. Cook the eggs for a few seconds, gently pushing the edges toward the center
  4. Fold the omelette in half, cook for 30–60 seconds, then serve.
