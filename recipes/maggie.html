maggie RECIPES

Description

Maggi is a popular instant noodle dish loved for its quick preparation and comforting flavor. With its signature masala spice packet, Maggi can be ready in just a few minutes, making it a go-to snack or light meal for students, busy folks, and food lovers alike.It’s easy to customize — you can enjoy it plain or mix in vegetables, cheese, or eggs to make it more filling and flavorful. Whether you're short on time or just craving something warm and satisfying, Maggi always hits the spot.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 packet of Maggi instant noodles
  • 1 ½ cups of water)
  • Maggi tastemaker masala (comes inside the packet)
  • Chopped vegetables (e.g., onions, tomatoes, peas, carrots)

STEPS

  1. Heat 1 tsp oil or butter in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Add chopped vegetables like onions, tomatoes, peas, or carrots. Sauté for 2–3 minutes until slightly soft.
  3. Pour in 1½ cups of water and bring it to a boil.
  4. Add the Maggi noodles and the tastemaker masala that comes with the packet.
  5. Cook for 2–3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the noodles are soft and most of the water is absorbed.
